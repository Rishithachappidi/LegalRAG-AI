Problem Statement
Quantum computing has emerged as a promising technology for solving complex
computational problems beyond the capability of classical computers. However, current Noisy
Intermediate-Scale Quantum (NISQ) devices suffer from hardware limitations such as noise,
decoherence, limited qubit connectivity, and gate errors.
Developers often rely on repeated executions and manual experimentation to evaluate circuit
performance and identify optimization opportunities.
Research Problem
There is no integrated AI-driven framework capable of automatically analyzing quantum
circuits, predicting their execution performance, and recommending optimization strategies
before execution.

Research Gaps Identified
Based on the literature survey, the following research gaps are identified:
Existing approaches mainly focus on circuit compilation or transpilation.
Very few studies predict circuit execution performance before execution.
Optimization techniques are mostly heuristic or compiler-based rather than AI-driven.
Current solutions do not simultaneously analyze circuit features, hardware characteristics,
and execution metrics.
No unified framework integrates performance prediction, optimization recommendation,
benchmarking, and visualization.
Limited benchmark datasets are available for training Machine Learning models on
quantum circuit optimization.
