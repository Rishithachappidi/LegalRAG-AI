Qubit initialization
❑Process of preparing a qubit in a specific, well-defined 
state, most commonly the |0⟩ state, before running a 
quantum algorithm
❑Various methods depending on the hardware platform – 
example: cooling the system to its lowest energy state to 
prepare |0⟩, or applying microwave or laser pulses to 
prepare |1⟩ by flipping |0⟩
Initialization is a crucial first step for any quantum 
algorithm, as the results depend entirely on the initial 
state of the qubits.
